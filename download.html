How to install

  1. Tap the download button above
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or Downloads folder
  3. If asked, allow "Install from this source" — this is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store
  4. Tap Install, then open Sona Play from your home screen

Sona Play is distributed directly rather than through an app store. Your device may show a security check before installing — this is expected for any app installed this way.
