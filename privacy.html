Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Sona Play ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication details, handled securely through our authentication provider (Supabase). We do not collect your name, phone number, or payment information unless you explicitly provide it for a premium feature.

We store your app activity to power app features: songs you like, your listening history, followed artists, and playlists. This is used only to personalize your experience within the app.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide personalized recommendations (Trending, For You, followed artists)
  • To let you resume your library and liked songs across sessions

Third-Party Services

Sona Play streams audio from publicly available sources. We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Supabase — authentication and database storage
  • Google/YouTube — source of streamed audio content

These providers have their own privacy policies governing data they process on our behalf.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not display third-party advertisements in the app.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account and associated data at any time from within the app's Settings page. Once deleted, your account data is permanently removed from our systems.

Children's Privacy

Sona Play is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
